# Experiment Design

Optimize for information gained, validity, feasibility and cost, not obligatory research stages.

## Design considerations (apply as relevant)
- Specify the research question and competing predictions if claiming a mechanism.
- Define independent experimental units, nesting, repetitions and dependencies.
- Define interventions, controls, metrics, randomization/blocking and likely confounds.
- Distinguish correct beliefs, updated plans, chosen actions and tool execution outcomes.
- Include null results, alternative explanations, a small pilot and scaling criteria.
- Record prompts, versions, task/sample selection, budgets, stopping rules and leakage risks.
- Be explicit whether the result establishes association, performance gain or a causal mechanism.

Next actions may include experiment, diagnostic probe, trace audit, targeted literature review, rerun, rollback, plan revision, or deferral. Never force an explore/execute binary.

Deliver a minimal actionable plan with what different outcomes imply. Read-only by default: obtain authorization before paid calls, mutations or releasing data.
