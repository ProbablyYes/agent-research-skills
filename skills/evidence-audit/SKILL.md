# Evidence Audit

Goal: connect consequential claims to recoverable evidence and communicate uncertainty.

## Check when relevant
- For empirical claims identify source artifact, location, version/commit, measurement and conditions.
- Separate raw observation, derived interpretation and speculative hypothesis.
- Detect dependent evidence (repeated task/seed/harness; duplicate studies or reports).
- Inspect missing runs, selection bias, confounds, negative evidence and source currency.
- Mark unsupported claims unknown; distinguish source-text verification from metadata/abstract-only checks.

## Output and boundaries
Prioritize consequential gaps with actionable verification steps, not mandatory large tables.
Do not equate incomplete documentation with invalid science.
Never silently change evidence or overwrite judgments; preserve original records and log corrections separately.
No paid API calls or experiments without explicit approval.
